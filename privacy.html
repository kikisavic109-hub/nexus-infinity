Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This page explains what the NEXUS INFINITY Discord bot (“the bot”) stores, why, and how to get it deleted. The bot is run by the owner of the NEXUS INFINITY Discord server.

What the bot stores about Discord members

  • Discord IDs of members, the server, its channels and roles
  • XP, level and activity counters; chosen titles; an optional bio and favourite anime (set by the member); the member’s anime watchlist
  • Support tickets (subject, topic, status), moderation cases with their reasons, and event sign-ups

Message contents are not stored, apart from a short fingerprint of a member’s last message (to spot repeated spam). When the protection system removes a message, an excerpt goes to the staff log channel in the server, and ticket transcripts are posted there for the staff.

What the bot stores from TikTok

Only if the server owner connects their own TikTok account:

  • the account’s TikTok ID and display name
  • the access tokens TikTok issues for the connection, stored encrypted
  • for each edit posted in the server: the video’s number of views, likes, comments and shares

The bot asks TikTok only for the permissions user.info.basic (profile name) and video.list (public videos and their numbers). It does not read messages, followers or private videos, and it never posts anything.

Why

To run the community features (levels, moderation, tickets, events, anime alerts) and to show the numbers of the owner’s TikTok edits on the edit posts in the server.

Where and how long

  • The data lives in the bot’s database on its server at Fly.io in Frankfurt, Germany, with automatic backups on the same server (kept about two weeks).
  • Discord data is kept while the bot runs in the server; members can ask for theirs to be deleted.
  • The TikTok tokens are deleted when the connection is ended. The numbers already shown stay on the posts until those are deleted.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or used for ads. Data only goes where it is needed to run the bot: Discord (the platform itself), TikTok (requests for the connected account), AniList (anime searches, without personal data) and Fly.io (hosting).

Your choices

  • Ask the server team for a copy or the deletion of your data: open a ticket in the NEXUS INFINITY Discord server.
  • The TikTok connection can be ended at any time with /tiktok disconnect or by removing the app’s access in TikTok’s settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the current version.
